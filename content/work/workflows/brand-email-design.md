# Brand Email Design (Design.md)

For a lean starter kit I'd prioritize: **general notification (catch-all), status update, invoice, proposal delivery, and cold outreach** — those five cover most day-to-day sends and establish the patterns (card, button pair, mono/status line, signature) the rest can reuse.

## Core Patterns to Establish
- **Card**: Clean bordered container for key content
- **Button pair**: Primary + secondary CTA buttons (inline or stacked)
- **Mono/status line**: Monospace or subtle status bar for metadata (date, ID, amount)
- **Signature**: Consistent professional block with name, title, email, phone, website (see owner contact block)

Templates sent to hodgedomain@google.com for review. Each uses the hodge@agentmail.to inbox, full HTML with inlined styles, plain text fallback, and the approved signature.

See `assets/brand/` for any supporting assets (logos, colors).
